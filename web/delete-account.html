Ultra MAX

Delete your setup

Permanently delete an Ultra MAX setup and the active account data associated with its token.

What is deleted

  • Your Ultra MAX setup, profiles and saved configuration.
  • Stored API/OAuth credentials and connected-service settings held with that setup.
  • The recovery-email link registered to that token.
  • Install fingerprints and UltraPlay Plus/supporter entitlement associated with that token.

This cannot be undone

After deletion, the token will stop working and the setup cannot be recovered from the live service. Limited information can remain temporarily in protected server logs or disaster-recovery backups and is not used for normal service operation.

If you cannot access the setup password, contact support@ultramax.vip for a manual deletion request. Privacy details are available in the Ultra MAX Privacy Policy.
